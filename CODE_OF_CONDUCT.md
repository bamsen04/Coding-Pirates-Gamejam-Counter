# Code of conduct

This project is made for a Coding Pirates game jam, with kids and young people in the room. Everyone who takes part here, in issues, pull requests, comments or chat, is expected to keep it a friendly place.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. People are here at different levels, and asking a question is always fine.
- Give feedback on the code, not the person. Say what could be better and why.
- Assume good intent, and say so when you don't understand something.
- Keep it suitable for all ages.

## What is not okay

- Insults, harassment, bullying or discrimination of any kind.
- Sexual language or images, or unwelcome attention.
- Sharing other people's private information.
- Anything that would be out of place in front of the kids at the jam.

## What happens

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may ask someone to leave the project's spaces if it keeps happening.

To report a problem, message Johan on Slack. It will be handled privately.
